# Itties trait set

A lightweight trait specification for the character collection. The seven categories below have six options each, for **279,936 theoretical combinations** before rarity rules or compatibility constraints. These are design traits; they do not imply a final NFT supply or rarity distribution.

| Category | Options |
|---|---|
| Body finish | Classic Red; Polka-Dot Pink; Sky Blue Striped; Floral; Stealth Matte Black; Neon Acid |
| Eraser band | Standard Pink; Blackout; Crimson Red; Electric Blue; Gold Metal Band; Neon Purple |
| Eyes | Confident; Wide-Eyed; Designer Shades; Wild Stare; Cyber Visor; Wink |
| Mouth | Grin; Smirk; Feral Snarl; Bubble Gum; Gold Grill; Neutral |
| Expression | Chill; Hype; Fierce; Glitch; Focused; Relaxed |
| Accessories | None; Puffer Coat; Bomber Jacket; Denim Vest; Gold Joshi Medallion; Dollar Chain |
| Motion effects | Static; Neon Glow Pulse; Particle Dust; Glitch Overlay; Floating Bob; Gold Shimmer |

## Trait boundaries

- The character remains a pencil with arms; no legs.
- Body finish, eraser band, eyes, and mouth are visual layers on the same base character.
- Expression can modify the pose of the face without replacing the selected eyes or mouth.
- Accessories sit over the body and should not hide the face or pencil silhouette.
- Motion effects are optional animation or render effects; they do not change the underlying character geometry.
- Assign exact counts and rarities only after the total collection supply and compatibility rules are decided.
