# Reference expressions and gestures

Source: reference.png supplied by the user. Built-in imagegen generated male-atlas.png and female-atlas.png.

Prompt set: Extract six frontal expression sprites on transparent backgrounds, arranged in a 3-by-2 atlas: Chill friendly eyes and small cheerful open smile; Hype wide eyes and delighted open grin; Fierce determined eyes, angry brows and clenched tooth grin; Glitch one wink and laughing smile; Focused furrowed brows and concentrated smirk; Relaxed half-closed eyes and calm small smile. Male and female variants share these moods; female has delicate lashes and glossy natural lips. No pencil, head, skin patch, hands, clothing, eyewear, labels or glow.

Registered by scripts/build-reference-expression-gestures.py with independent eyes/mouth layers and a minimum twelve-pixel eye-to-mouth gap. Each expression selects the correspondingly named 3D hand gesture. Glitch is a wink and finger guns; Relaxed has half-closed eyes and hands behind the head. All gloves use three fingers and one thumb.
